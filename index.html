About

A business background with a technical working rhythm.

Augusto is a Data & CRM Analyst based in Belo Horizonte, Brazil, with experience across banking, CRM systems, business intelligence and analytics.

His background combines business and technology: a Bachelor’s Degree in International Business in Brazil, a Master’s Degree in Information Technology in Australia, and hands-on experience working with data, customer platforms and reporting inside financial services.

At BOQ Group, he worked with Power BI, SQL, DAX and Microsoft Dynamics 365, supporting stakeholders across business and technical teams. He also worked directly with Deloitte and EY on strategic initiatives, and was recognised as Employee of the Month after delivering a lead tracking solution covering 111 bank branches.
