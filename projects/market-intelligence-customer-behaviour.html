Context

See the market around each branch, not only internal performance.

BOQ Group operated across a large Australian branch network. Leadership, Marketing, Retail, and branch stakeholders needed a stronger way to understand how each branch's customer portfolio compared with the demographic profile of its surrounding market.

Internal performance could show who the bank already served. External demographic and postcode-level data could show who lived in the local area. Bringing those views together created a more useful basis for branch and market strategy.

Business problem

Connect customer composition with local market potential.

Stakeholders needed to compare a branch catchment's wider population with the bank's actual customer portfolio. The business questions spanned demographics, languages, cultural backgrounds, age groups, household patterns, product holdings, and possible gaps in market reach.

  • Understand who lived around each branch or postcode area.
  • Compare external demographics with existing customer profiles and product holdings.
  • Identify customer groups or product opportunities that appeared under-represented.
  • Support more relevant local messaging, engagement, and branch planning.
  • Make complex multi-source analysis usable for non-technical leaders.

My role

Translated multi-source data into an executive-ready market view.

I supported the analysis and reporting layer, helping combine internal CRM and customer portfolio data with external demographic and postcode reference sources. My role required understanding the business questions behind the analysis, structuring comparisons at the right geographic level, and presenting the findings in a form branch and leadership stakeholders could use.

Core analytical questionHow does the customer base the bank currently serves compare with the broader population and opportunity in each local market?

Solution

A customer portfolio and market intelligence decision layer.

The reporting framework mapped internal customer and product information to postcode and branch geography, then aligned it with public demographic datasets. Power BI gave stakeholders a consistent way to move from a national view into local market comparisons without exposing unnecessary customer-level detail.

The result supported exploration of portfolio mix, demographic alignment, geographic gaps, and local product or engagement opportunities across the branch network.
