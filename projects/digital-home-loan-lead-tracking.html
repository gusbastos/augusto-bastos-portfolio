Context

A new digital channel needed a reporting standard.

BOQ Group was launching and expanding digital home loan journeys across its banking brands. Leads were being generated through websites, digital campaigns, app journeys, paid media, and marketing activations, but the business did not yet have a structured way to track those leads end to end.

The work began around ME Bank and Virgin Money Australia activity and later expanded toward the broader Bank of Queensland environment. The initial version was delivered in approximately two months, then evolved through further releases for additional brands, states, reporting needs, and business questions.

Business problem

Connect acquisition activity to what happened next.

The visibility gap went beyond missing reports. Digital Marketing, leadership, regional teams, and relationship stakeholders needed a consistent way to understand where leads came from, how they entered CRM, how follow-up progressed, and how different brands, states, and channels were performing.

  • Trace lead sources across digital journeys and campaign activations.
  • Connect paid media and marketing activity with customer acquisition outcomes.
  • Compare performance by brand, state, channel, and business area.
  • Give branch and relationship teams clearer visibility over CRM follow-up.
  • Create a repeatable, executive-ready reporting standard.

My role

Scoped and developed the reporting solution end to end.

I worked across Data Analyst, CRM analyst, BI developer, and business analysis responsibilities. I gathered stakeholder requirements, helped define the reporting logic, understood how campaign and lead data behaved across systems, and translated those needs into an evolving Power BI solution.

I also supported iterative releases as the framework expanded across brands and geographies, balancing detailed operational questions with the concise view leadership needed.

Working positionI operated as an analytics partner between Digital & Marketing, CRM data, reporting technology, and the business teams responsible for lead follow-up.

Solution

A governed decision-support layer for lead performance.

The framework brought CRM, campaign, lead, and paid media information into a structured Power BI reporting layer. DAX measures and data preparation logic turned activity from multiple sources into views that stakeholders could use to monitor lead flow, channel performance, follow-up, and campaign effectiveness.

Rather than treating each request as a one-off report, the solution established reusable logic that could accommodate additional brands, states, and evolving business questions.
